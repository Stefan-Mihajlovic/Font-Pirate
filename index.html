About font previews

Google Fonts previews load from Google. Your preview text stays on this device. Other website fonts may use a local fallback; their CSS values are preserved.

Using the inspector

Pick a font, then click text. Or select text and choose Identify font from the right-click menu. Esc cancels. Browser pages and PDFs are not supported.

Storage and backups

Pairings stay on this device. Back up your library before removing the extension. Up to 200 pairings.
